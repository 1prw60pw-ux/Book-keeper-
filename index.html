Meet Patricia

The bookkeeper who actually picks up the phone

Hi, I'm Patricia Woods. I keep the books for small-business owners, solopreneurs, contractors, and local shops — the people who wear every hat in their business and shouldn't have to wear this one too.

I'm a Texan from start to finish: born in Dallas, raised on Colorado mountain winters from the age of nine, drawn to California once I'd shoveled my lifetime quota of snow, and finally back home to Texas — where I intend to stay. Moving that many times teaches you a useful skill: how to take a box of chaos, sort it, label it, and turn it into a home. That is, more or less, my entire job description.

Numbers are my happy place. I'm the person who hunts down a seventeen-cent discrepancy because "close enough" isn't how I work. But what my clients tell me they value most isn't the precision — it's the patience. I never rush you, I never talk down to you, and I'll explain your numbers as many times as it takes until they make sense.

When I'm not in a ledger, I'm usually writing — I'm an author with books of my own. Writing and bookkeeping are closer cousins than people think: both reward discipline, structure, and sweating every small detail until the whole thing reads clean. It also means that when I explain your finances, you get plain English from someone who works with words for a living.

The rest of my time belongs to my husband, a fishing rod, and every dog within petting distance — I've never met an animal I didn't like. Fishing taught me patience. Dogs taught me loyalty. My clients get the benefit of both.

“My job is to do what's best for you — not what's fastest for me. We go at your pace, always.” — Patricia Woods, Owner

Good questions

Things people usually ask before they call

If your question isn't here, just ask — there's no such thing as a silly question about your own money.

How much does bookkeeping cost?

It depends on the size and state of your books, which is why I never quote blind. After our free consultation you'll get a clear, flat monthly price in writing — no hourly meter running, no surprise invoices. If the scope ever changes, we talk about it first.

My books are a mess. Honestly, years behind. Can you still help?

Yes — and you'd be surprised how many of my favorite clients started exactly there. Catch-up work is a specialty of mine, and there is no judgment here, ever. We'll untangle it one month at a time until you're current, and then keep you that way.

Do you replace my CPA or tax preparer?

No — I make them love you. A bookkeeper keeps your records clean all year; your CPA uses those records to file your taxes. I hand your CPA a tidy, reconciled, fully organized package, which usually means faster filing and fewer billable hours on their end.

What software do you work with?

QuickBooks is my home turf — I set it up, clean it up, and manage it day to day. If you're on something else (or on a shoebox of receipts), tell me what you've got and we'll work out the best path. You don't need to have anything figured out before we talk.

Is my financial information safe with you?

Completely. Your records are handled with strict confidentiality, shared only with people you authorize (like your CPA), and we'll set up a simple, secure way to exchange documents from day one. Your business stays your business.
